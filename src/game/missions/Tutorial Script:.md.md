Tutorial Script:

- Your current course is shown by the blue line.
- You can change course by performing burns
    - prograde: [A] applies thurst in your current direction
    - retrograde: [W] applies thrust opposite your current velocity
    - Left/right burns: apply thrust at a 90deg angle to your current velocity
    - Hold shift to perform a heavy burn. The dotted line shows the trajectory of a heavy burn.

Coast through the first 3 markers, then perform a prograde burn to get into a higher orbit.

Coast through the next 3 markers, then perform a retrograde burn to get into a lower orbit.

Place 5 markers at different points around the system and have the player go after them one at a time.

This completes basic manouver training. Let's move on to the next system.

Get enough velocity to escape, and get to the next system.

Quick stop at fuel asteroid.

On to brontes moons, navigating the moon field is the next challenge.

Next, Phaser training.
    - When phasers are armed, they will automatically fire on all locked targets in range.
    - Press [F] to arm/disarm phasers. A dashed red line means armed, a dotted line means they are safe.
    - Phasers can take out enemy torpedoes provided that the scanners have enough time to lock on to the incoming torpedo.
    - If weapons systems are prioritized, the range and power of the phasers are significantly increased.

    Take out the enemy torpedo launcher on the dark side of the aurelia moon.
